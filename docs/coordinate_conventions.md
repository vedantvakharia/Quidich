# Field and Coordinate System Conventions

Origin: The origin `(0,0,0)` is positioned exactly at the geometric center of the pitch.

Pitch Ends:
	FE: Far End (top of the pitch in the diagram).
	NE: Near End (bottom of the pitch in the diagram).





 Axes:

 Y-axis: Runs parallel to the length of the pitch, pointing positively towards the Far End (FE).

 X-axis: Runs perpendicular to the pitch, pointing positively towards the right side of the field.

 Z-axis: Points upwards, perpendicular to the ground plane (representing height).







# Camera Layout (7 Cameras)



The system uses 7 cameras (C1–C7) placed around the boundary, all pointing inwards toward the pitch:



 C1: Positioned straight down behind the Near End (NE), facing directly up the Y-axis toward the Far End.

 C2 \& C3: Positioned on the left side of the field, pointing right along the positive X-axis toward the pitch.

 C4: Positioned straight up behind the Far End (FE), facing directly down the Y-axis toward the Near End.

 C5 \& C6: Positioned on the right side of the field, pointing left along the negative X-axis toward the pitch.

 C7: Positioned in the bottom-right quadrant, pointing diagonally up-left toward the center of the pitch.
